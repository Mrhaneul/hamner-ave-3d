# 2655 Hamner Ave — 3D design draft

Interactive Three.js model of the proposed church building at 2655 Hamner Ave, Norco, CA (APN 127210020, lot about 49′ × 552′).

**Live:** https://mrhaneul.github.io/hamner-ave-3d/

## What it shows

- 7,000 sq ft first floor and 4,000 sq ft mezzanine on a 25′-wide footprint, centered on the lot with a 12′ drive lane on the north and south sides
- One continuous gable roof: 24′ eave, 6:12 pitch, about 30′ ridge (the high vault from the master plan deck)
- Room layout scaled from the deck's area tables (pages 3 and 4)
- Exterior, cutaway, longitudinal section and per-floor views, toggleable dimensions, and room info on hover
- Approximate surroundings traced from a printed aerial; not surveyed

Sliders adjust lane widths, floor areas and building position, and everything recomputes live.

## Run locally

Open `index.html` in a browser. It loads Three.js r128 from jsDelivr, so it needs an internet connection.

This is a planning draft, not a construction document.
